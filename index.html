Research

From atomic models to catalytic performance.

My research combines theoretical simulation with experimental validation to guide the design of non-precious-metal electrodes. I use first-principles thinking and Gibbs free-energy analysis to understand OH*, O*, and OOH* intermediates during water splitting.

Current interests include weak-alkaline water electrolysis, metal-air batteries, and photo-electrocatalytic coupling for sustainable energy conversion.

Concept

Star effect

Proposed a scale-sensitive catalytic phenomenon centered on local field construction, accelerated electron transfer, and size effect.

Materials

Transition-metal chalcogenides

Explored early-late dual transition-metal tellurides and chalcogenides as advanced catalysts for overall water splitting.

Single atoms

Cobalt on N-doped carbon

Designed cobalt single-atom catalysts on N-doped carbon nanoboxes and introduced the idea of a reaction sub-center.
