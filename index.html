Research

Small-scale science.
Real-world impact.

My research combines theoretical simulation with experimental validation to guide the design of catalytic materials and reaction pathways. I am especially interested in how light, plasma, and data-driven models can work together to improve CO2 conversion.

Current interests include photocatalysis, plasma catalysis, CO2 utilization, machine learning, and sustainable energy conversion.

01 / Light

Photocatalysis

Exploring light-driven catalytic materials and charge-transfer pathways for sustainable chemical conversion.

02 / Energy

Plasma catalysis

Studying plasma-assisted catalytic reactions where energetic species activate stable molecules under milder conditions.

03 / Carbon

CO2 utilization

Designing catalytic materials and reaction pathways to convert carbon dioxide into useful chemicals and fuels.

04 / Intelligence

Machine learning

Using data-driven methods to connect material descriptors, reaction performance, and catalyst design for CO2 conversion.
